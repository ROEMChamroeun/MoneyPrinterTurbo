## The mistakes, most important first

1. **Most of the content isn't yours.** One video is another creator's video with your dub on top. Another is a
   Serey Mun song that was posted as "original sound". TikTok's rules say it doesn't recommend reposted or unoriginal
   content on For You, and a song with copyright can also be muted or held back. This is the biggest problem.
2. **They were posted at 1–3 AM.** TikTok first tests a new video on a small group of people. At that hour the Khmer
   audience is asleep, so the test gets no reaction and the video stops spreading.
3. **The account is brand new and posted 3 videos in under 2 hours.** It has no history yet, so TikTok holds back
   while it learns what the account is about.
4. **The videos are too long for a new account.** Videos of 1:37 and 2:11 need viewers who already follow you. On the
   lyric video, most viewers stopped at 0:36.
5. **The three videos are about three different things:** nature, a love song, and life's purpose. TikTok can't tell
   who to show them to. The bio says "learning & useful little things", so stay on that one topic.
6. **`#fyp` does nothing.** It doesn't help a video reach anyone.

The "Contains AI-generated media" label on the tree video is not the problem. TikTok requires that label and doesn't
punish it.

## What to do next

- **Make your own videos.** Use your own narration over your own edit, or over royalty-free footage. Don't put your
  voice on top of someone else's whole video. For a dub, ask the creator for permission first.
- **Post once a day at a good hour:** around 11:00–13:00 or 19:00–22:00, Cambodia time. One good video a day for
  1–2 weeks works better than a burst on the first night.
- **Keep videos at 20–45 seconds** while the account grows, and say the most interesting line in the first 1–2 seconds.
- **For songs, use the official sound** from TikTok's music library instead of uploading the audio yourself.
- **Use 2–3 specific Khmer hashtags** about the topic instead of `#fyp`.
- **Wait 24–48 hours before you judge a video.** Once a video passes 100 views, check Studio → Analytics →
  "Traffic source" to see whether For You is showing it.
