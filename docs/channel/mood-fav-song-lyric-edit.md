## The mood in one line

Quiet heartbreak: regret and betrayal set against a calm, golden sea. The picture is peaceful and beautiful while the
words hurt, and that contrast is where the feeling comes from. It never gets loud or dramatic. It stays at one low,
aching level for the whole minute.

## The music

These are measurements, not listening (Claude can't hear audio).

- **Soft:** the drums sit about 7 dB under the melody instruments.
- **Tempo:** about 112 BPM.
- **Key:** it sits right between D major and F# minor, which is the usual bittersweet ballad sound.
- **Flat loudness:** it stays between −10 and −11.5 dB in every 5 seconds, with no build-up and no drop.
- **Singing:** mostly 220–370 Hz, a range of about 10 semitones.

## The picture

- **No people or faces.** Only the sea at sunset: slow waves, glitter on the water, soft focus, warm amber and gold.
- **Few cuts.** Four clips in 59 s, joined with slow 0.8 s dissolves on lyric changes.
- **The colour tells the story.** A pale, hazy sunset, then golden wet sand, then glitter on the water, then a deep
  orange sun sinking into the horizon, then black. The day ends as the song reaches "die for you".

- Small white Khmer, centred, one short phrase at a time, with a soft shadow.
- A handwriting-style font, which makes it feel personal, like a diary.
- Spoken lines have quotation marks; sung lines don't.
- No title, emoji or hashtags on screen.

## Recipe for a similar short

1. **Hook:** 2–3 spoken lines of confession or regret in quoted captions, ending on one short word such as "sorry".
2. **Song:** a slow, soft Khmer heartbreak song from the other person's side. Use its strongest 40 seconds.
3. **Footage:** nature with no people (water, sky, golden hour, slow motion), three to five clips, getting darker
   towards the end.
4. **Captions:** one phrase at a time, centred, small, white with a soft shadow. Use a handwriting font if a Khmer
   one with a clear licence is available; otherwise Google Sans at weight 400.
5. **Ending:** an unfinished line with "…" and a fade to black.
6. **Length:** 60 s or under.
