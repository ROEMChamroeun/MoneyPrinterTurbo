### What we know about the 2026 Water Festival

- The government **cancelled the Phnom Penh festival** for the second year in a row, in a circular signed June 25. It
  cited the Thai border situation and displaced families. Provinces may hold their own festivals, and the public holiday
  still stands. ([Phnom Penh Post](https://phnompenhpost.com/national/phnom-penh-water-festival-suspended-government-focus-on-border-crisis-displaced-civilians/),
  [Khmer Times](https://www.khmertimeskh.com/501932337/govt-cancels-phnom-penh-water-festival/),
  [Cambodianess](https://cambodianess.com/article/cambodia-calls-off-phnom-penh-water-festival-for-second-consecutive-year))
- Most calendars give the holiday as **Nov 23–25**, but one gives Nov 24–26.
  ([Officeholidays](https://www.officeholidays.com/holidays/cambodia/water-festival-ceremony),
  [Calendarific](https://www.calendarific.com/holiday/cambodia/water-festival))
- Siem Reap (Nov 23–25), Takeo, and a Kampong Cham race on Oct 22–26 are announced **only in TikTok posts**. No news
  source confirms them yet, so the video gives no dates.

## The pick: "Cambodia has a river that flows backwards"

The Tonle Sap river changes direction twice a year. In the rainy season the Mekong rises and pushes water up into the
lake, and the lake grows about 4–5 times. Around the month of Kadeuk (ខែកត្តិក) the river turns and drains back into
the Mekong, and that turn is when Khmer people hold the Water Festival.

Why this topic:

- It uses the festival trend while it's rising, and it will still work every year after this one.
- It fits the bio: it teaches one surprising thing, and the viewer comes away proud of their own country.
- It is our own work, with our own narration and our own animated map. Nothing is reposted.
- It stays clear of politics. The video never mentions the Phnom Penh cancellation or the reason for it.

Facts in the script are kept to ranges that the sources agree on. The dry-season lake covers about 2,500–3,000 km² and
can reach about 10,000–16,000 km² in the wet season
([Wikipedia](https://www.Wikipedia.com/wiki/Tonle_Sap), [JICA report](https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/11582509_05.pdf),
[Wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tonle_Sap_Lake)).

## Look and sound

- **1080×1920, filling the whole frame**, with no letterbox or blurred edges. Khmer kinetic captions in Google Sans
  700, kept above y=1420 so TikTok's buttons don't cover them.
- **The animated map is the main visual.** Draw it as an SVG web page: Cambodia's outline, the Mekong, the Tonle Sap
  river and the lake, with river and lake shapes from Natural Earth (public domain). Render it frame by frame with the
  same method as `storage/ai-problem-first-edu/render.mjs`. The map is the part no other Khmer account has.
- **Stock clips are only for mood.** Pixabay has almost nothing from Cambodia: 1 clip for "tonle sap" and 9 landscape
  clips for "phnom penh". Use its portrait clips of the full moon (75 found), lanterns, fishing and rivers. **Don't use
  its dragon-boat clips.** They show Chinese boats, and Khmer viewers will notice. For the festival shots, use Wikimedia
  Commons photos with a CC licence and credit them in the caption, or your own photos.
- **Voice:** `khmer-voice-artist` or `flower-wilted-speaker-b`. Speaker B reads about 12 characters a second, which
  gives the 43-second length above.
- **Music:** export the video with the voice only. In the TikTok app, add a calm sound from TikTok's music library at
  low volume. That keeps the music licensed and also lets the sound help people find the video.
